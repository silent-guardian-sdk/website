The Silent Crisis

Undetected,
Unprotected.

In the critical seconds of a seizure, silence is the greatest threat.

Current monitoring systems are locked behind expensive paywalls and unreliable cloud connections. For the 50 million people living with epilepsy, a missed alert isn't just a technical failure—it's a life-threatening gap. While emergency alerts must utilize standard mobile networks, the life-saving detection algorithm itself must remain entirely local.
We are building the alternative: Always active, locally processed, and completely subscription-free.

The Engine

Technical
Workflow.

01. Biometric Capture

The wearable sensor continuously tracks raw physical motion and heart rate data.

02. Signal Filtering

The system filters out everyday movement noise to isolate true physiological patterns.

03. On-Device AI

A local machine learning model processes the data directly on the device to detect seizure signatures.

04. Alert Dispatch

If a seizure is detected, the system instantly sounds a local alarm, notifies caretakers, and dispatches emergency SMS and SOS alerts as a fallback if any system anomalies occur.

System Hardware

Two Devices.
One Local System.

1

The Wearable Sensor

Continuous on-body physiological tracking. The compact, low-power, durable wearable band captures physical acceleration and heart rate metrics, performing real-time filtering directly on-device before wireless streaming.

2

The Local Processing Dock

The physical local AI engine. Receives pre-filtered telemetry data wirelessly from the band. It processes advanced machine learning classifiers completely offline to trigger immediate alarms, notifying caretakers and remote SMS and sos alerts.
